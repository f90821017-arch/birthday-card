The Angry Bird Emerges

I remember the first time I really saw you. Not just looked—saw.

There was this intensity in everything you did. The way you got mad about the smallest things. The way you needed attention like oxygen. The way your energy filled a whole room and made everyone feel something.

Most people would call it dramatic. I called it you.

And I was gone.

Ok Ooo

You say it all the time. Ok ooo.

When you're pretending to agree but you're not really. When you're done with something but also not done. When you're getting mad but also kind of finding it funny. When you're saying yes but meaning maybe.

It's your whole language in two syllables.

And I've learned to hear what you're really saying underneath. I've learned your codes. I've learned you.

Ok ooo means I love you in a way only we understand.

The Mad About Everything Era

You get mad at everything. I'm not exaggerating—everything.

You get mad when I don't text back fast enough. You get mad when I do something without asking you first. You get mad at the weather, at traffic, at your phone, at the way I'm looking at you right now.

And the thing is: I love it. Because underneath all that anger is someone who cares deeply. Someone who doesn't do half measures. Someone who feels everything at full volume.

Your anger isn't a bug. It's a feature. It's proof that you're alive, that you're present, that you're real.

You're my angry bird, and I wouldn't change a single feather.

Attention Required

You need attention. Constant, specific, undivided attention.

You need me to notice when you're upset. You need me to ask you about your day. You need me to check on you, to respond to your messages, to make you feel like you matter more than whatever else is happening.

And here's the thing: you do matter more. You always have.

I don't give you my attention because I have to. I give it to you because you're the most interesting person in my world. Because your thoughts are worth hearing. Because the way your mind works fascinates me.

You don't need fixing. You need witnessing. And I'm here for that.

Why You Matter

Your positive energy isn't just a vibe. It's a force.

You walk into a room and something shifts. People feel it. I feel it. The world feels a little brighter because you're in it, even when you're mad about something, even when you're demanding attention, even when you're being difficult.

You make me want to be better. Not because I'm trying to impress you—though I am. But because being around someone who feels things this deeply, who cares this much, who refuses to settle for ordinary or half-hearted—it changes you.

You've changed me.

And I'm grateful for every Ok ooo, every moment of drama, every time you demanded I show up more fully.

To My Angry Bird

I love you. Not in a quiet way. Not in a convenient way. Not in a way that fits neatly into words.

I love you as you are—intense, demanding, dramatic, full of contradictions and fire and light.

I love the way you love. I love how you feel. I love that you won't let me half-ass anything, including being with you.

On your birthday, I want you to know: you are not too much. Your anger isn't a problem to solve. Your need for attention isn't neediness—it's you knowing what you deserve.

You deserve someone who shows up. Someone who sees you. Someone who loves the angry bird as much as the joyful one.

That's me. That's always been me.
